# 1. Product Vision

## 1.1 Mission

Make chess the most loved learning game for children aged 5–15 by turning every rule into a story, every
concept into a game, and every game into progress, and by building a pipeline of strong young players who
can go on to compete and become titled masters.

## 1.2 Goals

| Goal | How we know we achieved it |
|------|----------------------------|
| Every child learns **all the rules correctly** | 95% of children who finish Level 3 pass the "Rules Champion" test: legal moves, check, mate, stalemate, castling, en passant, promotion |
| Children **want to come back** | D7 retention ≥ 40%, D30 ≥ 25%; average of 3 or more sessions a week |
| Children **actually get stronger** | Measurable rating growth on puzzles and against bots; transfer to over-the-board play (assessed by teachers and coaches) |
| Parents and teachers **trust it** | No ads, no open chat, privacy by design, clear progress reports |
| Strong kids **have a path to mastery** | Levels 8–10 cover tournament play, notation, clocks, analysis and preparation, plus a referral path to coaches and rated events |

## 1.3 Audiences and personas

| Persona | Age | Needs | Design response |
|---------|-----|-------|-----------------|
| **Mia, the pre-reader** | 5–6 | Can't read fluently, short attention span (5–10 min), loves characters and animals | Full voice narration, no text needed, big touch targets, characters with faces, instant rewards |
| **Omar, the curious explorer** | 8–9 | Reads simple sentences, loves quests, collecting and levelling up | Adventure map, quests, collectibles, short text plus voice, friendly bots |

| **Parent** | — | Is it safe? Is my child learning? How long do they play? | Parent dashboard, screen-time controls, weekly progress email, no ads |
| **Teacher or coach** | — | Run a class, assign lessons, see who is stuck | Classroom mode, assignments, heat-map of mastery per concept, projector mode |

## 1.4 The three presentation tracks

The **content** (skill levels 1–10) is shared. The **presentation** adapts to the child's age. Parents can
override the track, and the app suggests a switch when a child's behaviour suggests it (for example, a
5-year-old who is reading every hint).

| | **Little Knights** (5–7) | **Explorers** (8–10) | **Academy** (11–15) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Guide character | **Pip the Pawn**, a brave little pawn who dreams of becoming a queen | Pip plus **Captain Rook** | **Coach Nova**, a sharp young chess coach (Pip appears as an easter egg) |
| Pieces | Characters with faces and expressions (Story Set) | Stylised toy-like pieces | Realistic tournament Staunton-style pieces |
| Text | None required; everything is voiced | Short sentences plus voice | Full text, voice optional |
| Session length | 5–10 min | 10–15 min | 15–30 min |
| Rewards | Stars, stickers, dancing pieces | XP, badges, collectibles, map unlocks | Ratings, streaks, leaderboards among friends and class, titles |
| Challenge pacing | Errorless learning: wrong moves bounce back with a gentle hint | Hints after a mistake, retry | Hints cost points; timed modes |
| Notation | Hidden (squares are shown with pictures and colours) | Coordinates introduced (a–h, 1–8) | Full algebraic notation, PGN export |

## 1.5 Design principles

1. **Show, don't tell.** Every rule is first *seen* in 3D, then *tried*, then *named*.
2. **One idea per lesson.** Lessons last 3–7 minutes and teach exactly one concept.
3. **Play within 30 seconds.** The child touches a piece within 30 seconds of opening the app.
4. **Mistakes are safe.** Wrong moves are learning moments, never punishments. No "Game Over" screens for young children.
5. **Real chess, always.** Simplified mini-games never teach anything that is wrong in real chess.
6. **A real-board feel.** Weight, wood, sound and shadow make it feel like a physical set, which helps transfer to over-the-board play.
7. **Calm, not addictive.** No loot boxes, no pay-to-win, no manipulative streak pressure, built-in break reminders.
8. **Safe by design.** No ads, no open chat, no personal data from children, parental consent (COPPA, GDPR-K, UK Age Appropriate Design Code, UAE PDPL).
9. **Accessible to all.** Colour-blind-safe highlights, full voice-over, adjustable speed, reduced-motion mode, multilingual (including right-to-left Arabic).

## 1.7 Feature pillars

1. **Adventure map and lessons:** a 3D world of kingdoms, one per level, containing story lessons.
2. **Mini-games:** more than 30 skill games (Pawn Wars, Star Collector, Knight's Quest and others).
3. **Puzzle Gym:** adaptive tactics and checkmate puzzles, rated per child.
4. **Play Zone:** games against bot personalities that make human mistakes, pass-and-play with family, safe friend games through invite codes.
5. **Coach's Corner:** post-game review in kid language ("Oops! Your knight was left alone here").
6. **Hall of Legends:** stories of great players (Morphy, Capablanca, Judit Polgár, Kasparov, Carlsen and others) with their famous moments set up as puzzles.
7. **Parent and teacher hub:** progress, assignments, classroom mode, printable certificates.
8. **AR Board** (later phase): place a 3D board on a real table.

## 1.8 Success metrics (North Star)

**North Star:** *weekly "mastered concepts" per active child*. This captures both learning and engagement.

Supporting metrics:

- Lesson completion rate, mini-game replay rate.
- Puzzle rating growth per month.
- Bot ladder progress.
- D1/D7/D30 retention, sessions per week, average session length (compared with the age target).
- Parent NPS and teacher adoption (classes created, assignments completed).
- Over-the-board transfer: results from in-class assessments and club partners.
